Time Series 2022

Energy Demand Forecasting

Accurate 30-day ahead electricity demand forecasting using advanced time series models to optimize grid management and energy trading.

↗ View on GitHub ← All Projects
The Problem

Balancing supply and demand

Electricity grid operators must constantly balance power generation with consumption. Overestimating demand leads to wasted energy and higher costs, while underestimating can result in power outages and grid instability.

The goal was to create a highly accurate forecasting model that accounts for daily and seasonal seasonality, weather impacts, and holiday effects to predict hourly demand 30 days into the future.

Approach

Time Series Intelligence

01 / Data Aggregation

Multi-source Data

Combined historical energy consumption data with external regressors like temperature forecasts and a custom holiday calendar.

02 / Seasonality Analysis

Decomposition

Performed time series decomposition to isolate trend, daily/weekly seasonality, and residual noise for better model understanding.

03 / Hybrid Modeling

Prophet + LSTM

Developed a hybrid approach using Facebook Prophet for capturing strong seasonalities and an LSTM neural network to model non-linear residuals.

04 / Backtesting

Walk-forward Validation

Evaluated model robustness using time series cross-validation with a rolling origin to simulate real-world forecasting performance.
